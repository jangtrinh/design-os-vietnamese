# Glossary — Landing Page Conversion & UX Wording (EN → VI)

Vietnamese digital marketing and landing page conversion copy has established conventions. Translating raw English word-for-word produces unnatural calques that native visitors instantly clock as machine output, eroding trust and killing conversion rates.

**Format Contract:** The table under `<!-- machine-readable: glossary -->` is parsed directly at runtime by `validate_copy.py`.
- Column 1 (`❌ Calque`): Blocklist of machine-translation tells.
- Column 2 (`✅ Native`): Recommended production phrasing. Multiple allowed choices are separated by ` / `.
- Adding or editing a row in this table automatically creates or updates a linter rule.
- Do not make a blocked phrase a substring of any recommended alternative.

## 2. High-Frequency Calque Disambiguation

1. **`Học thêm` vs `Tìm hiểu thêm`:**
   - English "Learn more" on buttons translates to `Học thêm` only if you run a school or tutoring academy.
   - For software, enterprise services, real estate, and consumer products, the reader is not studying for an exam; they are discovering information. Use `Tìm hiểu thêm` or `Khám phá ngay`.

2. **`Liên lạc` vs `Liên hệ`:**
   - `Liên lạc` denotes technical transmission or social keeping-in-touch (*"giữ liên lạc"*).
   - In commercial inbound marketing, requesting consultation is strictly `Liên hệ` (*"Liên hệ phòng kinh doanh"*, *"Liên hệ tư vấn"*).

3. **`Chứng thực` vs `Cảm nhận khách hàng`:**
   - `Chứng thực` in Vietnamese legal terminology means notarial signature certification (*"chứng thực chữ ký"*).
   - Customer feedback banners and social proof carousels must use `Cảm nhận khách hàng` or `Đánh giá từ người dùng`.

4. **`Chính sách riêng tư` vs `Chính sách bảo mật`:**
   - `Riêng tư` translates personal intimacy (privacy of thoughts or personal life).
   - The statutory and technical term under Vietnamese law (Luật An toàn thông tin mạng & Luật 91/2025/QH15) is strictly `Chính sách bảo mật` or `Bảo vệ dữ liệu cá nhân`.
