# Primary Source Dossier 05: Multi-Industry Expansion — 7-Tier Pedagogy (Mầm Non -> Tiến Sĩ), Healthcare (Luật KCB 2023), Legal Contracts (BLDS 2015 & LTM 2005), Hospitality (Luật Du Lịch 2017), and HR (BLLĐ 2019)

## 1. Full 7-Tier Vietnamese Pedagogy Continuum (Mầm Non -> Sau Đại Học)
- Quote-ID [claim-edtech-continuum-1]: "Under Consolidated Document 01/VBHN-BGDĐT on the Early Childhood Education Curriculum (Chương trình Giáo dục Mầm non), nurseries (Nhà trẻ, 3–36 months) develop 4 domains while kindergartens (Mẫu giáo, 3–6 years) develop 5 domains (Phát triển thể chất, Phát triển nhận thức, Phát triển ngôn ngữ, Phát triển tình cảm và kỹ năng xã hội, Phát triển thẩm mỹ); preschool assessment strictly forbids numeric scores or A/B/C/Học lực rankings, evaluating children solely against end-of-age developmental expectations (Đạt / Chưa đạt) and WHO growth charts."
- Quote-ID [claim-edtech-continuum-2]: "MOET Circular 27/2020/TT-BGDĐT governs Primary School assessment (Grades 1–5), establishing 3 subject achievement levels ('Hoàn thành tốt — Hoàn thành — Chưa hoàn thành'), 3 core quality/competency levels ('Tốt — Đạt — Cần cố gắng'), and 2 year-end commendation titles ('Học sinh Xuất sắc' and 'Học sinh Tiêu biểu hoàn thành tốt trong học tập và rèn luyện'), abolishing 'Học sinh Tiên tiến' at the primary level."
- Quote-ID [claim-edtech-continuum-3]: "Across Vietnam's 8-level National Qualifications Framework (Khung trình độ quốc gia Việt Nam — VQF, Decision 1982/QĐ-TTg), Vocational Education (Law on Vocational Education & Circular 04/2022/TT-BLĐTBXH) organizes training by occupational modules (mô-đun nghề), Undergraduate Education (Level 6, Circular 08/2021/TT-BGDĐT) culminates in a 'Khóa luận tốt nghiệp' or 'Đồ án tốt nghiệp', Master's Education (Level 7, Circular 23/2021/TT-BGDĐT) culminates in a research 'Luận văn thạc sĩ' or applied 'Đề án thạc sĩ', and Doctoral Education (Level 8, Circular 18/2021/TT-BGDĐT) requires 'Nghiên cứu sinh (NCS)' to complete doctoral specialized topics ('Chuyên đề tiến sĩ'), international publications (Web of Science SCIE/SSCI/A&HCI or Scopus), and defend a 'Luận án tiến sĩ'."
- Quote-ID [claim-edtech-continuum-4]: "Under Decision 37/2018/QĐ-TTg, 'Giáo sư (GS.)' and 'Phó Giáo sư (PGS.)' are state-recognized academic ranks/titles (chức danh / học hàm), whereas 'Tiến sĩ (TS.)', 'Thạc sĩ (ThS.)', and 'Cử nhân (CN.)' are academic degrees (học vị); Vietnamese academic style writes rank before degree ('GS.TS. Nguyễn Văn A', 'PGS.TS. Trần Thị B') and forbids conflating rank with degree ('học hàm Tiến sĩ', 'học vị Giáo sư')."

## 2. Healthcare, Pharmaceuticals, Dietary Supplements (TPCN), and Electronic Medical Records (EMR)
- Quote-ID [claim-healthcare-med-1]: "The Law on Medical Examination and Treatment 2023 (Luật Khám bệnh, chữa bệnh số 15/2023/QH15, effective 01/01/2024) officially replaces 'Chứng chỉ hành nghề khám bệnh, chữa bệnh' with 'Giấy phép hành nghề khám bệnh, chữa bệnh' (valid for 5 years per renewal cycle) and mandates strict patient health data confidentiality under Article 10."
- Quote-ID [claim-healthcare-med-2]: "Under Decree 15/2018/NĐ-CP (Article 27) and Circular 05/2018/TT-BYT, all health supplement (Thực phẩm bảo vệ sức khỏe — TPCN) advertising and product copy must include the verbatim disclaimer 'Thực phẩm này không phải là thuốc và không có tác dụng thay thế thuốc chữa bệnh', and must never claim therapeutic cure ('điều trị dứt điểm', 'chữa khỏi hoàn toàn') or use patient thank-you letters or physician/medical-facility imagery for promotional endorsement."
- Quote-ID [claim-healthcare-med-3]: "Circular 32/2023/TT-BYT on Electronic Medical Records (Bệnh án điện tử — EMR) and Circular 52/2017/TT-BYT on outpatient prescriptions require explicit drug active ingredient (INN), concentration, dosage per intake, frequency per day, administration route, meal timing ('trước ăn' / 'sau ăn'), and emergency red-flag return criteria ('Dấu hiệu cần tái khám ngay')."

## 3. Civil, Commercial, Consumer Protection, and PDPD Contract Drafting
- Quote-ID [claim-legal-contracts-1]: "Article 301 of the Commercial Law 2005 (Luật Thương mại số 36/2005/QH11) caps contractual breach penalties ('phạt vi phạm') in commercial contracts at 8% of the value of the breached contractual obligation, contrasting with civil contracts under Article 418 of the Civil Code 2015 (Bộ luật Dân sự số 91/2015/QH13) where penalty levels are freely agreed by the parties."
- Quote-ID [claim-legal-contracts-2]: "Article 20 of the Labor Code 2019 (Bộ luật Lao động số 45/2019/QH14) abolished seasonal labor contracts ('hợp đồng lao động mùa vụ'), permitting only indefinite-term contracts ('hợp đồng lao động không xác định thời hạn') and fixed-term contracts not exceeding 36 months ('hợp đồng lao động xác định thời hạn không quá 36 tháng')."
- Quote-ID [claim-legal-contracts-3]: "Article 25 of the Law on Protection of Consumers' Rights 2023 (Luật Bảo vệ quyền lợi người tiêu dùng số 19/2023/QH15) invalidates standard-form contract clauses that unconditionally exempt the business from statutory liability; additionally, Vietnamese statutory citations must follow ascending-to-descending or specific-to-general Vietnamese legislative hierarchy ('Điểm a Khoản 2 Điều 15') rather than Anglo-American 'Điều 15.2(a)'."

## 4. Hospitality, Tourism, Aviation, and F&B Service Standards
- Quote-ID [claim-hospitality-travel-1]: "Article 50 of the Law on Tourism 2017 (Luật Du lịch số 09/2017/QH14) recognizes tourist accommodation ratings strictly from 1 star to 5 stars based on official appraisal decisions by the Vietnam National Authority of Tourism or provincial tourism departments, prohibiting self-proclaimed '6 sao' or '7 sao' classifications and unaccredited star claims."
- Quote-ID [claim-hospitality-travel-2]: "In Vietnamese hospitality and fine-dining F&B pricing, net prices ('Giá đã bao gồm phí phục vụ và thuế GTGT') must be clearly distinguished from plus-plus ('++') prices by explicitly disclosing the service charge percentage (typically 5% phí phục vụ) and value-added tax percentage (8% or 10% thuế GTGT)."
- Quote-ID [claim-hospitality-travel-3]: "Under Ministry of Transport Circular 14/2015/TT-BGTVT and Circular 19/2023/TT-BGTVT on non-refundable advance compensation and passenger obligations in air transport, flight delay or cancellation notices must state an explicit apology, the verified operational or weather cause, updated departure schedule, and passenger meal/accommodation/refund/re-routing entitlements."

## 5. Human Resources, Recruitment, Offer Letters, and Labor Code Compliance
- Quote-ID [claim-hr-labor-1]: "Article 8 Clause 1 of the Labor Code 2019 strictly prohibits employment discrimination based on gender, marital status, pregnancy, age, ethnicity, religion, or disability in recruitment announcements (JD), while Article 17 forbids employers from retaining original identity cards, diplomas, or certificates or requiring monetary/asset deposits from employees as a condition of employment."
- Quote-ID [claim-hr-labor-2]: "Under Articles 25 and 26 of the Labor Code 2019, probation periods may not exceed 180 days for enterprise managers (Luật Doanh nghiệp), 60 days for positions requiring college (Cao đẳng) or higher degrees, 30 days for intermediate/technical roles, and 6 working days for other jobs, and probation salary must be at least 85% of the official wage for that job."
