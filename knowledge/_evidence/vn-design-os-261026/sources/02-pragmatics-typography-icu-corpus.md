# Primary Source Dossier 02: Pragmatics, Register Matrix, DESIGN:OS Typography Metrics, and CLDR/ICU Engineering

## 1. Vietnamese Register Matrix and Kinship Pragmatics
- Quote-ID [claim-register-pragmatics-1]: "Vietnamese lacks a neutral universal second-person pronoun equivalent to English 'you'; every speaker-addressee pair encodes age, social distance, institutional authority, and transactional context."
- Quote-ID [claim-register-pragmatics-2]: "The DESIGN:OS Vietnamese Register Matrix defines 18 distinct registers across digital product UI (ux-microcopy), SaaS/consumer landing pages, enterprise B2B, banking/wealth management, administrative/government (Nghị định 30/2020/NĐ-CP), and healthcare."
- Quote-ID [claim-register-pragmatics-3]: "In ux-microcopy (buttons, menus, toasts, form validation, empty states), the optimal register drops pronouns entirely in action labels ('Lưu thay đổi', 'Tạo dự án') and uses 'bạn' / 'của bạn' only in conversational feedback, avoiding the pompous 'Quý khách' inside compact UI controls."
- Quote-ID [claim-register-pragmatics-4]: "Automated pronoun validators (PRO001, PRO002) must exempt Sino-Vietnamese and native compound words where 'bạn' or 'quý' is a bound morpheme rather than a second-person pronoun, including 'bạn bè', 'bạn đọc', 'bạn hàng', 'bạn đời', 'kết bạn', 'tình bạn', 'quý giá', 'quý hiếm', 'quý báu', 'quý trọng', 'quý mến', and fiscal quarters 'Quý I', 'Quý II', 'Quý III', 'Quý IV', 'Quý 1'–'Quý 4'."

## 2. Calque, Passive Voice, and Sino-Vietnamese Balance
- Quote-ID [claim-register-pragmatics-5]: "English-to-Vietnamese calques degrade natural cadence: 'Làm ơn' in UI prompts ('Làm ơn nhập email') carries a begging connotation and must be replaced with 'Vui lòng nhập email' or direct imperative 'Nhập email'; 'Của bạn' at the start of a noun phrase ('Của bạn đơn hàng') violates Vietnamese head-first noun phrase syntax."
- Quote-ID [claim-register-pragmatics-6]: "Vietnamese distinguishes adversity passive 'bị' (negative outcome: 'Tài khoản bị khóa', 'Thanh toán bị từ chối') from beneficiary passive 'được' (positive/neutral outcome: 'Đơn hàng đã được xác nhận') and active/state 'đã' ('Hệ thống đã lưu thay đổi'); writing 'Đơn hàng bị hủy thành công' or 'Tệp của bạn đã bị tải lên' is a high-severity pragmatic error (SYN001)."

## 3. DESIGN:OS Vertical Metrics and Typography for Stacked Diacritics
- Quote-ID [claim-typography-metrics-1]: "Vietnamese uppercase and lowercase vowels with circumflex or horn/breve plus a tone mark above (Ấ, Ầ, Ẩ, Ẫ, Ế, Ề, Ể, Ễ, Ố, Ồ, Ổ, Ỗ, Ớ, Ờ, Ở, Ỡ, Ứ, Ừ, Ử, Ữ) extend 25% to 38% above the Latin cap-height, while the dot-below tone mark (Ạ, Ặ, Ậ, Ẹ, Ệ, Ị, Ọ, Ộ, Ợ, Ụ, Ự, Ỵ) extends below the baseline into the descender zone."
- Quote-ID [claim-typography-metrics-2]: "Applying English-tuned tight line-height values (such as Tailwind leading-none 1.0 or leading-tight 1.25 on body text) or placing overflow: hidden on single-line headings clips the top hook/tilde of 'Ẩ/Ễ' and the bottom dot of 'Ệ/Ộ'; DESIGN:OS enforces a minimum line-height of 1.5 for body text and 1.28 to 1.35 for display headings."
- Quote-ID [claim-typography-metrics-3]: "Web font loading via next/font/google or @font-face must explicitly include the 'vietnamese' subset (U+0102-0103, U+0110-0111, U+0128-0129, U+0168-0169, U+01A0-01A1, U+01AF-01B0, U+1EA0-1EF9, U+20AB); omitting it causes browser fallback to system fonts exclusively on accented characters ('ransom-note' glyph mismatch)."
- Quote-ID [claim-typography-metrics-4]: "Vietnamese translations expand horizontal string length by +20% to +35% compared to English ('Save' 4 chars -> 'Lưu thay đổi' 12 chars); UI components must avoid fixed pixel widths and bind final two-syllable compound words with U+00A0 non-breaking spaces to prevent orphaned syllables."

## 4. CLDR vi-VN Locale Formatting and ICU MessageFormat Engineering
- Quote-ID [claim-locale-formatting-1]: "Under CLDR vi-VN and Vietnamese accounting conventions, thousands are separated by dots ('.') and decimal fractions by commas (','); VND amounts use no decimal places ('1.250.000 ₫' or '1.250.000 VND'), while compact shorthand uses 'tr' (triệu), 'tỷ', or 'nghìn'/'ngàn' rather than English '1.5M VND' (NUM003)."
- Quote-ID [claim-locale-formatting-2]: "Unit symbols m² and m³ must use Unicode superscripts (U+00B2, U+00B3) rather than ASCII 'm2'/'m3' (NUM004), but validator rules must exempt hardware processor tokens such as 'Apple M2', 'MacBook Air M2', 'chip M2 Pro', 'M2 Max', 'M2 Ultra', and 'iPad M2'."
- Quote-ID [claim-locale-formatting-3]: "Vietnamese has a single CLDR plural category ('other'); ICU MessageFormat strings in vi-VN must define only the '=0' (optional exact zero) and 'other' branches, and multi-line ICU blocks containing English 'one', 'two', 'few', or 'many' selectors must be flagged by a balanced-brace parser (ICU001)."
