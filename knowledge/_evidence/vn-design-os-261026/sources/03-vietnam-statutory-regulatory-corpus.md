# Primary Source Dossier 03: Vietnam Statutory Compliance, Thông tư 99/2025/TT-BTC Finance, and MOET EdTech Regulations

## 1. Advertising, Data Protection, Promotions, and Administrative Law
- Quote-ID [claim-statutory-compliance-1]: "Article 8 Clause 11 of the Law on Advertising 2012 (Luật Quảng cáo số 16/2012/QH13), as amended by Law No. 75/2025/QH15 (effective 01/01/2026), prohibits advertising that uses 'nhất', 'duy nhất', 'tốt nhất', 'số một' or words of equivalent meaning without statutory supporting documentation, and mandates explicit sponsorship disclosure by influencers (KOL/KOC)."
- Quote-ID [claim-statutory-compliance-2]: "Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP on Personal Data Protection (PDPD) invalidates pre-ticked checkboxes and implied 'By continuing you agree' banners; Article 11 requires explicit consent covering data types, processing purposes, third parties, and data subject rights, while Article 20 mandates dual verification of both the child's consent (for ages 7 to under 16) and the parent or legal guardian's consent."
- Quote-ID [claim-statutory-compliance-3]: "Decree 81/2018/NĐ-CP (Article 6 & 7) as amended by Decree 128/2024/NĐ-CP caps standard commercial promotional discounts at 50% of the goods/service price immediately preceding the promotion, permitting up to 100% discount only within government-organized concentrated promotional programs (Tháng khuyến mại tập trung quốc gia)."
- Quote-ID [claim-statutory-compliance-4]: "Decree 91/2020/NĐ-CP on anti-spam telecommunications requires prior opt-in, advertiser prefix tagging ('[QC]'), and explicit free opt-out syntax ('Soạn TC gửi...') for promotional SMS/ZNS/emails, and strictly forbids disguising promotional incentives inside transactional Zalo Notification Service (ZNS) templates."
- Quote-ID [claim-statutory-compliance-5]: "Decree 30/2020/NĐ-CP on Clerical and Archival Work (Công tác văn thư) standardizes the national emblem header ('CỘNG HÒA XÃ HỘI CHỦ NGHĨA VIỆT NAM / Độc lập - Tự do - Hạnh phúc'), reference number format ('Số: .../CV-...'), recipient block ('Kính gửi:'), and closing authority abbreviations ('TM.', 'KT.', 'TL.', 'TUQ.') across Công văn, Tờ trình, Quyết định, Thông báo, and Biên bản."

## 2. Corporate Accounting (Thông tư 99/2025/TT-BTC) and Banking/Fintech Compliance
- Quote-ID [claim-finance-tt99-1]: "Circular 99/2025/TT-BTC issued by the Ministry of Finance on 27/10/2025 supersedes Circular 200/2014/TT-BTC for fiscal years beginning on or after 01/01/2026, officially renaming 'Bảng cân đối kế toán' (Form B 01-DN) to 'Báo cáo tình hình tài chính' (Statement of Financial Position) in alignment with IAS 1 / IFRS."
- Quote-ID [claim-finance-tt99-2]: "Circular 99/2025/TT-BTC introduces Account 215 ('Tài sản sinh học' / Biological Assets), Account 332 ('Phải trả cổ tức, lợi nhuận' / Dividends and Profits Payable), and Account 82112 ('Chi phí thuế TNDN bổ sung theo quy định về thuế tối thiểu toàn cầu' / Pillar Two Global Minimum Tax Expense)."
- Quote-ID [claim-finance-tt99-3]: "In Vietnamese retail banking, securities, and e-wallet interfaces, bonds ('trái phiếu doanh nghiệp'), mutual fund certificates ('chứng chỉ quỹ'), and gold/crypto assets must never be labeled 'tiền gửi tiết kiệm' (savings deposit) or 'cam kết lãi suất cố định không rủi ro', and credit copy must clearly distinguish flat nominal interest ('lãi suất trên dư nợ gốc ban đầu') from effective reducing-balance interest ('lãi suất trên dư nợ giảm dần')."

## 3. MOET Education Assessment (Thông tư 22/2021/TT-BGDĐT & KNLNN 6 Bậc)
- Quote-ID [claim-edtech-moet-1]: "MOET Circular 22/2021/TT-BGDĐT governs K-12 student assessment under the 2018 General Education Curriculum (GDPT 2018), replacing legacy 'Học lực: Giỏi / Tiên tiến / Yếu / Kém' labels with the 4-tier classification 'Tốt — Khá — Đạt — Chưa đạt' for both conduct ('Kết quả rèn luyện') and learning outcomes ('Kết quả học tập')."
- Quote-ID [claim-edtech-moet-2]: "Under Circular 01/2014/TT-BGDĐT, Vietnam's 6-level Foreign Language Proficiency Framework (Khung năng lực ngoại ngữ 6 bậc dùng cho Việt Nam — KNLNN 6 bậc, assessed via VSTEP) maps Bậc 1–6 to CEFR A1–C2; EdTech platforms must neither equate non-accredited internal quiz scores with official VSTEP certificates nor promise 'Cam kết 100% đỗ đại học / đạt IELTS 8.0'."
