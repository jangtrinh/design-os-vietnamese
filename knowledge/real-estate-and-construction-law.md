# Vietnamese Real Estate, Property Development & Construction Law: Law on Real Estate Business 29/2023/QH15, Land Law 31/2024/QH15, and Housing Law 27/2023/QH15

## Purpose

Defines authoritative statutory guidelines for real estate project commercialization, off-plan residential development (`nhà ở hình thành trong tương lai`), land tenure disclosures (`sổ đỏ`, `sổ hồng`), apartment area measurements, and construction procurement (BOQ) under the 2023–2024 real estate legislative overhaul.

## When to Use / When NOT

- **When to Use:**
  - Drafting, localizing, or auditing off-plan sales contracts (`Hợp đồng mua bán nhà ở hình thành trong tương lai`), deposit agreements (`Thỏa thuận đặt cọc`), project sales brochures, and construction BOQ schedules in Vietnam.
  - Verifying legal compliance under Law on Real Estate Business 2023 (Luật Kinh doanh bất động sản số 29/2023/QH15), Land Law 2024 (Luật Đất đai số 31/2024/QH15), and Housing Law 2023 (Luật Nhà ở số 27/2023/QH15).
- **When NOT to Use:**
  - Everyday consumer retail promotions or standard software B2B service contracts that do not involve real property or construction works.

## Core Knowledge Content

### 1. Statutory Deposit Cap for Off-Plan Property (`REA001`)

Under the Law on Real Estate Business 2023, project developers are strictly prohibited from mobilizing unauthorized capital under disguised deposit agreements or goodwill booking fees.
Article 23 Clause 5 of the Law on Real Estate Business 2023 (Luật Kinh doanh bất động sản số 29/2023/QH15, effective 01/08/2024) caps deposit amounts ('tiền đặt cọc') for off-plan properties ('nhà ở, công trình xây dựng hình thành trong tương lai') at 5% of the selling or lease-purchase price when the project meets all statutory conditions for commercialization.

### 2. Statutory Milestone Payment Caps (`REA002`)

Payment schedules for off-plan properties must strictly adhere to the phased completion thresholds set by law to safeguard buyer capital against project delays.
Article 25 of the Law on Real Estate Business 2023 limits off-plan property installment payments to a maximum of 30% of contract value on the first installment (inclusive of deposit), 70% prior to handover (or 50% for foreign-invested developers), and 95% prior to issuance of the Land Use Rights and Assets Attached to Land Certificate.

### 3. Land Tenure Accuracy, Carpet Area & 2% Maintenance Fund (`REA003`, `REA004`, `REA005`)

Marketing materials and sales contracts must never promise permanent residential title on commercial-service land, and must provide transparent apartment area and maintenance fund calculations.
Article 134 of the Land Law 2024 (Luật Đất đai số 31/2024/QH15) standardizes the official certificate title to 'Giấy chứng nhận quyền sử dụng đất, quyền sở hữu tài sản gắn liền với đất' and prohibits marketing commercial-service land projects (condotel, officetel, shophouse thương mại dịch vụ) as having permanent residential land tenure ('sổ đỏ lâu dài / sở hữu vĩnh viễn'); meanwhile, Articles 141 and 152 of the Housing Law 2023 (Luật Nhà ở số 27/2023/QH15) mandate explicit separation of carpet area ('diện tích thông thủy') from gross wall-center area ('diện tích tim tường') and separate disclosure of the 2% apartment maintenance fund ('kinh phí bảo trì phần sở hữu chung nhà chung cư 2%').

## Failure Modes (Mandatory)

1. **Deposit Exceeding 5% Cap (`REA001`):** Demanding deposit payments of 10%–20% or booking reserves prior to statutory commercialization approval.
2. **Payment Schedule Breach (`REA002`):** Collecting > 30% on first installment, > 70% before handover, or > 95% before certificate issuance.
3. **Misleading Commercial Land Tenure (`REA003`):** Claiming `sổ hồng vĩnh viễn` or `sở hữu lâu dài` for condotels, officetels, or commercial shophouses on 50/70-year leasehold land.
4. **Ambiguous Apartment Area (`REA004`):** Stating `diện tích căn hộ` without distinguishing usable carpet area (`diện tích thông thủy`) from wall-center gross area (`diện tích tim tường`).
5. **Unlawful Maintenance Fund Demands (`REA005`):** Overcharging > 2% maintenance fund or collecting maintenance fees prior to handover.
6. **Literal English Calques in Real Estate/BOQ (`REA006`):** Translating `green space` to `vùng xanh lá cây`, `real estate` to `tài sản thực`, `bill of quantities` to `danh sách số lượng`, or `rough handover` to `bàn giao thô ráp`.
